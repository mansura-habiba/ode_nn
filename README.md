# Research workspace

The previous CCNN research programme has been retired. Its experimental results, limitations, and decision history remain available in the [research wiki](wiki/README.md).

The active codebase no longer treats CCNN as the preferred approach. Future work on irregularly sampled time series, event-time state models, and revision/reconciliation should be independently justified against competitive baselines. Do not interpret the previous CCNN experiments as evidence of a general architectural advantage.

## Research workflow

See [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and the reusable [hypothesis-first research skill](.agent/skills/hypothesis-first-research/SKILL.md). Negative findings are valid outcomes; do not change benchmarks or hypotheses solely to obtain a positive result.
